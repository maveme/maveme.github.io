Hi there! I'm an Assistant Professor in Computer Science at VU Amsterdam and founder of the Analogue Intelligence Lab and Sports Intelligence Lab. My research focuses on end-user programming, domain-specific languages, and software environments for creative computing. My work sits at the intersection of Software Engineering and Human-Computer Interaction—building interactive systems, live-coding tools, and programming abstractions that empower people (from researchers to artists and educators) to build and express ideas through code.

Some of my research interests include:

  • Software engineering
  • Software evolution & maintenance
  • Creative computing
  • Sports analytics and data science
  • Software language engineering
  • Domain-specific languages

I'm also a guest researcher at SET group at TU/e and SWAT group at CWI.

Whether you're a fellow researcher, a student with a question, or just someone curious about my work, feel free to reach out and connect! ⚽️🎾
